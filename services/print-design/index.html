Print & Poster Design in Nairobi

Print still builds trust in person. We design posters, flyers, brochures, business cards and banners that look sharp and are prepared correctly for your printer.

A poster designer in Nairobi who understands print

Print has its own rules: bleed, resolution, colour mode and paper. We prepare every file so your printer in Nairobi gets exactly what they need, and your colours come out the way you expect.
