Logo Design in Nairobi & across Kenya

Your logo is the face of your business. We design clean, memorable logos for Kenyan businesses and startups that look sharp on a shopfront, a social profile, a website and a printed invoice.

A logo designer in Kenya who starts with your business

Good logo design begins with questions, not software. We learn what you sell, who you sell to and what makes you different, then turn that into a simple mark people remember.

Every concept is drawn to work at any size, from a favicon to a billboard, and in colour or black and white.

Why a professional logo matters

Customers in Nairobi and across Kenya meet your brand on WhatsApp, Instagram, signage and packaging long before they speak to you. A consistent, professional logo builds trust on every one of those surfaces.

A logo is also the foundation of your wider identity. If you need colours, fonts and guidelines to go with it, see our branding services, or explore our logo and branding portfolio.

What’s included

  • Research into your business, audience and competitors
  • Original logo concepts, not templates
  • Refinement rounds on your chosen direction
  • Colour palette and typography guidance
  • Full-colour, one-colour and reversed versions
  • Final files for print and web
