Who we are

BOLDINK DESIGNS is a creative design studio owned and led by Nelson Kariuki, a graphic designer based in Kenya. The studio creates bold, clean and modern visual designs that help businesses, organisations and individuals communicate with confidence.

Our approach is simple: bold ideas, stunning designs. We listen first, design with purpose and deliver files you can use straight away.

What we do

Nelson and BOLDINK DESIGNS offer graphic design, logo design, branding, website design and print design for startups, small businesses, events and growing brands in Nairobi and across Kenya.
